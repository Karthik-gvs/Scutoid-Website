    AI on your terms

    Two ways of working. Each team chooses.

    AI is not a separate product. It runs through every product, set per team, and within a team per role or process. It is designed so an agent can do any task a person can, through the same permissions, approvals and audit trail.

    Assisted

    Your team does the work. OANI helps.

    Forms, lists and reports, worked by people. A team setting turns AI help on or off. On adds suggestions, auto-fill, anomaly flags and document reading into drafts. Off sends no data to an AI model.

    Delegated

    OANI prepares the work. Your team approves it.

    For each routine task that meets its published accuracy level, you state an intent by text or voice, such as “raise purchase orders for this week’s shortfalls”. An agent prepares the work, a person approves it, and the decision is recorded. Nothing is auto-approved.

    A person stays accountable

    Nothing is filed or posted to the accounts without human approval.

    Explained and audited

    Every AI action shows its reason and source, and is kept in the audit trail.

    Accurate enough to trust

    Accuracy is measured for each type of action. If it drops below its published level, the task falls back to Assisted with an alert.

    No training on your data

    Your data is never used to train AI models.

    Included in the price

    Core AI, including the agents’ routine work, is planned to be included in the per-user price. Only unusually heavy use is metered, with caps you set.

    You can start with one agent

    Agents work alone or with the products, so a firm not yet using our products can begin with one.

    AI capabilities are in design. Delegated is offered for a task only once it meets its published accuracy level.

    Compliance AI

    AI-native, not bolted on.

    Four things Compliance AI does across the suite.

    Read

    Understands invoices, challans, certificates and drawings as they arrive, and turns them into structured records.

    Check

    Tests every transaction against the rules that apply — tax, statutory, quality and supplier — before it becomes a problem.

    Explain

    Shows the reason behind every flag in plain English, with a link to the source record.

    Prepare

    Drafts the correction, the return or the response. A person gives a signed approval before anything is final.
