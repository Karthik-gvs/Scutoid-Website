Mission

One connected platform, with AI as much as each team chooses.

We give growing discrete manufacturers one platform to run the whole business, with local compliance built in wherever they operate. AI does as much of the work as each team chooses, and people make every decision.

Services

Need hands-on help? Scutoid Technologies has you covered.

Two kinds of service, IT Services and Accounting Services, are delivered by our sister company, Scutoid Technologies Private Limited.

IT Services

Get the software set up and running

For your IT team and system owners.

  • Application solutions: set up, configure and extend Scutoid applications for the way your business works.
  • Integrations and data migration: connect Scutoid to the tools you already use and bring your data across.
  • Maintenance: keep your applications current, healthy and running as they should.
  • Support: answers and fixes from people who know your setup.
Accounting Services

Get the books done and filed

For your finance team and owners.

  • Bookkeeping: day-to-day entries, bill and payment posting, and bank reconciliation.
  • GST and TDS: preparing returns and deduction records for your review and filing.
  • Month-end close: reconciled accounts and a close checklist, every period.
  • Books set-up: chart of accounts and opening balances, including a move from Tally, Zoho Books, QuickBooks or Xero.

IT Services keep the software working. Accounting Services do the accounting work itself. You can take either one, or both.
